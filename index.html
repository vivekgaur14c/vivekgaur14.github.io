The Art of Indian
Palace Design

Drawing from centuries of royal architectural heritage, we infuse every project with the grandeur of India's most magnificent palaces. Intricate jali work, hand-carved sandstone, and majestic domes find new expression in contemporary luxury interiors.

Our designs honor the rich tapestry of Indian craftsmanship — from the mirror-work of Rajasthan to the teakwood carvings of Kerala — creating spaces that feel both regal and deeply personal.
